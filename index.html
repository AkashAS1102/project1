Education

Audio Glossary

Demystifying the tech jargon so you know exactly what you're listening to.

Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)

Microphones detect external sound waves and the headphones generate inverse waves to cancel the noise out before it reaches your ear.

Frequency Response

The range of bass, mids, and treble a speaker can produce. Humans generally hear 20Hz (low bass) to 20,000Hz (high treble).

Impedance

Measured in Ohms, it's the electrical resistance of the headphones. Lower impedance is better for phones, higher requires an amp.

Soundstage

The perceived spatial environment of the audio. A wide soundstage makes you feel like the band is playing in the room around you.

Lossless Audio

Audio files compressed without losing any data from the original studio master, providing bit-for-bit accuracy.

Dynamic Driver

The most common speaker mechanism. Uses electromagnetism to move a diaphragm, known for producing powerful bass.
